Your body’s own chemistry

Your brain and spinal cord release chemicals that work the brake or the accelerator at the gate. Your choices change which ones are active.

      Two paths after pain

      Pain→Worst-case thinking→Fear of movement→Avoidance→Less fit, low mood↺ more pain
      Pain→Curiosity, less fear→Gradual exposure→Recovery

      Your EP helps you move from the top path to the bottom one with education, gradual exposure and talking through unhelpful beliefs.

      Look back at the start

      Does pain mean something is damaged?
      What pain science says

      Sometimes. Pain and damage are related but not the same. You can have damage without pain, and pain without damage. Pain is a protector: a prediction of what could happen, not a report of what has happened.

      When is pain helpful?
      What pain science says

      When it protects tissue that is healing, like a fresh ankle sprain. Pain changes your behaviour so you give the area extra care while it repairs.

      When is pain unhelpful?
      What pain science says

      When it protects you after you no longer need protecting, such as after tissue has healed. Pain can be learned, and it can be relearned too.

      When to see a doctor promptly
      • Loss of sensation, or major weakness
      • Changes to bowel, bladder or sexual function
      • Pain after a major trauma, fall or collision
      • Significant redness, heat or swelling, or signs of infection
      • A history of cancer, or night pain that stops you getting back to sleep
      • Unexplained weight loss of more than 4 kg
